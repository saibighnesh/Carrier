Carrier

send a picture through a text-only chat

Turns an image into plain text you can paste into any messenger — even one that blocks photos, like in-flight chat. Add a password to lock it. Runs entirely on your device; nothing is uploaded.

1 · Pick an image Accepts JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF and AVIF. BMP, HEIC and HEIF can be chosen but only open if your browser can decode them.
Drop an image here, click to choose, or paste with Ctrl/Cmd+V JPG · PNG · WebP · GIF · AVIF — plus BMP/HEIC if your browser reads them — gets compressed to fit the text pipe

2 · Squeeze to fit
Higher quality is sharper but sends more text. 70%
Caps the longest edge in pixels. Smaller means less text to send.
Sets the per-message character limit for your chat app. Smaller limits split into more parts.
Halves the message count on apps that count characters. Both sides need a version of Carrier that understands it, and it makes SMS worse rather than better.

Adds extra parts so the recipient can rebuild missing ones without asking you to resend. Costs more messages.
